# Next steps

## Decisions needed before slide design

- Audience: internal leadership, partners, customers or a mixed room?
- Length: 10, 20 or 45 minutes?
- Language: English, Russian or both?
- Demo: live deterministic demo, recorded video or screenshots only?
- Orbit: in the room or not? If yes, use the collaborative framing, not a competitive one.
- Real data: may we use real task names, screenshots and numbers?

## Build plan

1. **Validate the story.** Walk `02-narrative.md` through with two or three people from the target
   audience. Cut anything they do not react to.
2. **Choose 10–12 slides.** The current arc is 14; the demo can absorb two.
3. **Write final slide copy.** One headline, at most three supporting lines. No paragraphs.
4. **Draw the missing diagrams.**
   - proof binding;
   - not a plugin;
   - comparison matrix;
   - built vs proposed.
5. **Build the PowerPoint** in this folder: `openjiuwen-works.pptx`.
6. **Rehearse the demo** with the fallback images. Record a backup video if the room is risky.
7. **Review for claims.** Every comparison honest, every proposed link marked proposed.

## Definition of done

- A person who saw the deck can explain why Works is not Orbit and not a harness.
- They can name the two gates and what each approves.
- They can say why "just add modules" changes the other product.
- They ask for a demo or a pilot.
- Every claim has a source in `06-sources.md` or a demo step.
