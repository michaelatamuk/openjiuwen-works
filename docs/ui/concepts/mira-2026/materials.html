Same bird. Fifteen materials.

These are material and art-direction studies, not 15 final mascots. The silhouette stays close to Mira; the visual language changes completely. Pick a material, not a pose. Directions 1–7, 12–15 need a real 3D/render pass; vector directions are marked.
