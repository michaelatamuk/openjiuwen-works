Rules of the system

  • One character, one job. Mira carries the proof; she does not decorate gates.
  • Ink for warmth, route for motion, seal for decisions. Never all three at once.
  • Small sizes use the glyph. No face, no scarf, no page below 44 px.
  • Red is a stamp, not a brand. One vermilion dot per surface.
  • No panda, dragon, lantern or red envelope. The story is the magpie, not a theme.
